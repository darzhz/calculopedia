Roof pitch is how steep a roof is. Roofers express it as rise per 12 inches of run — a "6/12 roof" rises 6 inches for every 12 inches it runs horizontally.

## The three ways to say it

1. **Pitch ratio (X-in-12)** — rise per 12 inches of run.
2. **Angle in degrees** — measured from horizontal.
3. **Percent grade** — rise ÷ run × 100.

## The math

    Ratio = rise ÷ run          (scaled to run = 12)
    Angle = arctan(rise ÷ run)  in degrees
    Grade = (rise ÷ run) × 100%

## Worked example

A roof that rises 6 inches over a 12-inch run:

    Ratio = 6-in-12
    Angle = arctan(6 ÷ 12) ≈ 26.6°
    Grade = (6 ÷ 12) × 100 = 50%

## What pitches mean in practice

| Pitch | Angle | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2/12–4/12 | 9–18° | Low slope — needs special roofing |
| 4/12–9/12 | 18–37° | Standard asphalt shingles |
| 9/12–12/12 | 37–45° | Steep — dramatic look, harder to walk |
| 12/12+ | 45°+ | Very steep — sheds snow well, costly |

A steeper roof sheds rain and snow better but costs more to build and is more dangerous to walk on.
