MAGI (Modified Adjusted Gross Income) gates eligibility for a long list of tax benefits, so getting it right matters.

## The formula

    MAGI = AGI + add-backs

Your **AGI** is gross income minus above-the-line deductions. For MAGI, the most common deductions you must add back are:

- Traditional **IRA** contributions
- **Student loan interest**
- Tax-exempt **foreign earned income** and housing
- Certain education-related deductions

## Worked example

An AGI of ₹12,00,000 with ₹50,000 of IRA contributions to add back:

    MAGI = 12,00,000 + 50,000 = ₹12,50,000

## Why MAGI matters

MAGI determines:

- **Roth IRA** contribution eligibility (income phase-outs)
- **Healthcare** premium tax credits and subsidies
- **Saver's credit** and some education credits
- **Medicare** premium surcharges

Being even slightly over a MAGI limit can cost you an entire benefit.
