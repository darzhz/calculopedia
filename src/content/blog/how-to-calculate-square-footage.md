Square footage is the universal measure of a home's size and the standard unit for buying flooring and paint. The calculation is one multiplication.

## The formula

    Square footage = length × width

Both dimensions must be in **feet**.

## Worked example

A room **10 ft × 12 ft**:

    10 × 12 = 120 square feet
    120 ÷ 10.7639 ≈ 11.15 square meters

## Metre conversion

Measured in metres? Convert each dimension (or the final area):

    1 m = 3.28084 ft
    1 m² = 10.7639 ft²

A **4 m × 3 m** room:

    4 × 3 = 12 m²
    12 × 10.7639 ≈ 129 square feet

## Planning materials

- **Flooring** — add ~10% for cutting waste: a 120 sq ft room needs ~132 sq ft of tiles or planks.
- **Paint** — one gallon covers roughly 350 sq ft per coat; one litre covers ~100–120 sq ft.
- **Rent/property** — Indian listings use sq ft, sq m or sq yards (1 sq yd = 9 sq ft).

## Irregular rooms

Split an L-shaped room into two rectangles, calculate each, and add. Total the sections of any odd shape this way.
