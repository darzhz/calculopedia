Cubic feet tell you how much space something takes up — a box, a fridge, a moving carton. It's the three-dimensional cousin of square footage.

## The formula

    Cubic feet = length × width × height

All three dimensions must be in **feet**.

## Worked example

A box **2 ft × 2 ft × 3 ft**:

    2 × 2 × 3 = 12 cubic feet

## Converting inches and centimetres

Measure in inches or centimetres? Convert each dimension first:

    inches   ÷ 12    → feet
    centimetres ÷ 30.48 → feet

A **24 in × 24 in × 36 in** box:

    24 ÷ 12 = 2 ft,  36 ÷ 12 = 3 ft
    2 × 2 × 3 = 12 cubic feet

## Quick conversion table

| 1 cubic foot | equals |
| --- | --- |
| 1,728 cubic inches | in³ |
| 0.0283 cubic meters | m³ |
| 28.317 liters | L |

So our 12 ft³ box holds about **340 liters**.

## Why it matters

- **Shipping** — carriers bill by dimensional weight (volume ÷ a divisor), so box size directly affects price.
- **Storage** — estimating how many boxes fit in a unit or vehicle.
- **Appliances** — refrigerator and water-heater capacity in cubic feet.
