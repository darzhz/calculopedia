Trying to decide between a salary offer and an hourly contract? Convert both to the same unit — an **hourly rate** — and the comparison becomes obvious.

## The formula

    Hourly rate = annual salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks per year)

## Worked example

**₹12,00,000 per year** at **40 hours/week, 52 weeks/year**:

    Hours per year = 40 × 52 = 2,080
    Hourly rate = 12,00,000 ÷ 2,080 ≈ ₹577/hour

That's about **₹4,615 per 8-hour day**, **₹23,077 per week** and **₹1,00,000 per month**.

## Tune the inputs

- **Weeks per year** — 52 assumes no unpaid leave. With 2 weeks unpaid, use 50: your effective rate rises to ~₹600/hour.
- **Hours per week** — a 45-hour week on a salary meant for 40 lowers your true hourly rate.
- **Paid time off** — if leave is paid, keep 52 weeks; the hours are still paid.

## Comparing a salary vs hourly contract

| | Salaried | Hourly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paid leave | Usually yes | Usually no |
| Benefits | Often included | Not included |
| Overtime | No | 1.5× common |
| Income gaps | None | Between gigs |

Add the cash value of benefits and paid leave to a salary offer before comparing. A ₹12,00,000 salaried job with paid leave generally beats ₹577/hour with unpaid gaps.

## Reverse it

Want the annual figure from hourly? Multiply by hours × weeks:

    ₹577 × 40 × 52 = ₹12,00,000
