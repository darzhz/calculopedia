TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) is every calorie you burn in a day: your resting metabolism, digesting food, and all activity. Eat exactly your TDEE and your weight stays put.

## The formula

    TDEE = BMR × activity factor

BMR uses the Mifflin-St Jeor formula:

    Men:    BMR = 10 × weight(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) − 5 × age + 5
    Women:  BMR = 10 × weight(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) − 5 × age − 161

## Activity factors

| Activity | Factor |
| --- | --- |
| Sedentary | 1.2 |
| Lightly active (1–3 days/week) | 1.375 |
| Moderately active (3–5 days/week) | 1.55 |
| Very active (6–7 days/week) | 1.725 |
| Extra active (physical job) | 1.9 |

## Worked example

A 30-year-old man, 175 cm, 75 kg, moderately active:

    BMR = 750 + 1093.75 − 150 + 5 ≈ 1,699 kcal
    TDEE = 1,699 × 1.55 ≈ 2,633 kcal

## Using your TDEE

- **Maintain** — eat your TDEE (2,633 kcal).
- **Lose** — eat ~500 below (≈ 2,133 kcal).
- **Gain** — eat ~500 above (≈ 3,133 kcal).

These are estimates — muscle mass, genetics and hormones shift real numbers. Track your weight for a few weeks and adjust by 100–200 kcal.
