Ovulation is the release of an egg, and it happens roughly **14 days before your next period** — which is why we count back from your cycle, not forward from your period.

## The formula

    Ovulation = period start + (cycle length − luteal phase)
    Fertile window = ovulation − 5 days to ovulation + 1 day
    Next period = period start + cycle length

## Worked example

Last period started 1 August 2026, 28-day cycle, 14-day luteal phase:

    Ovulation = 1 Aug + (28 − 14) = 15 August 2026
    Fertile window = 10–16 August 2026
    Next period = 29 August 2026

## Why the window is 6 days

- **Sperm** can survive up to **5 days** inside the body.
- The **egg** lives only **about 24 hours** after release.

So the conception window spans the five days before ovulation plus the day itself.

## Accuracy note

Cycle length varies. Basal body temperature tracking, cervical mucus, or ovulation test strips pinpoint ovulation far more precisely than a calendar estimate. This method is for information — not a substitute for medical advice or contraception.
