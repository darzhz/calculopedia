ROI answers a simple question: for every rupee I put in, how much did I get back?

## The formula

    ROI = (final value − cost) ÷ cost × 100

## Worked example

Invest ₹1,00,000 and it's now worth ₹1,50,000:

    ROI = (1,50,000 − 1,00,000) ÷ 1,00,000 × 100 = 50%

## Annualized ROI — the honest comparison

Plain ROI ignores time. Annualized ROI spreads the return over the years held:

    Annualized ROI = ((final ÷ cost)^(1 ÷ years) − 1) × 100

That same 50% over 5 years:

    ((1.5)^(1/5) − 1) × 100 ≈ 8.4% per year

## Compare this way

| Investment | Total ROI | Period | Annualized |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed deposit | 50% | 5 yrs | 8.4% |
| Stock | 50% | 2 yrs | 22.5% |

Same headline ROI, completely different performance. Use annualized ROI for comparisons.
