In Indian numerology, two numbers derived from your **date of birth** are used more than any others: your **Mulank** (root number) and your **Bhagyank** (destiny number).

## Mulank (Moolank) — the root number

Add the digits of your **birth day** until a single digit remains.

    Born on the 15th → 1 + 5 = 6 → Mulank = 6
    Born on the 29th → 2 + 9 = 11 → 1 + 1 = 2 → Mulank = 2
    Born on the 7th   → Mulank = 7

Your Mulank reflects your **core nature** — the traits you were born with.

## Bhagyank — the destiny number

Add the digits of your **full date of birth** (day + month + year) and reduce.

For **15 June 1995**:

    15 + 6 + 1995 = 2016
    2 + 0 + 1 + 6 = 9 → Bhagyank = 9

Your Bhagyank points to the direction your **life path** takes.

## What the numbers are used for

- **Compatibility** — practitioners compare Mulank/Bhagyank between people (the numerological cousin of FLAMES).
- **Auspicious timing** — picking dates or numbers aligned with your root number.
- **Self-reflection** — understanding your tendencies and strengths.

As with all numerology, treat the readings as food for thought, not prediction. It's a belief system, not a science — but it's great fun and a lovely conversation starter.
