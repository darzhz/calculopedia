Tracking your hours is simple subtraction — until overnight shifts and breaks get involved. Here's the full method.

## The basic formula

    Hours worked = (clock out − clock in) − break time

## Worked example

Clock in at **09:00**, out at **17:00**, with a **30-minute** lunch:

    17:00 − 09:00 = 8 hours
    8 − 0.5 = 7.5 hours worked

That's **7 hours 30 minutes** — or **7.5** in the decimal form most payroll systems use.

## Overnight shifts

Shift crosses midnight? Add 24 hours to the clock-out before subtracting.

    In: 23:00, Out: 07:00
    07:00 + 24:00 = 31:00
    31:00 − 23:00 = 8 hours

## Payroll decimals

Convert minutes to decimal by dividing by 60:

| Worked | Decimal |
| --- | --- |
| 7 h 30 m | 7.5 |
| 8 h 15 m | 8.25 |
| 6 h 45 m | 6.75 |

## Accurate tracking tips

- Record the **exact minute** of each clock in/out.
- Subtract only **unpaid** breaks — short paid breaks usually count as work.
- Sum each day for the week. If overtime applies (often 1.5× above 40 hours or 8/day), separate regular from overtime hours.
