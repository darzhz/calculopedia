Zone 2 is the easy-but-purposeful aerobic zone: conversation-level effort that builds your endurance engine and burns fat as its primary fuel.

## The percentage method

    Max HR = 220 − age
    Zone 2 = 0.6 × max HR  to  0.7 × max HR

## The Karvonen method (more personal)

    HRR = max HR − resting HR
    Zone 2 = (0.6 × HRR + resting HR) to (0.7 × HRR + resting HR)

Karvonen accounts for your resting heart rate, which reflects your fitness level — fitter people have lower resting HR and higher zone 2 numbers.

## Worked examples

A 35-year-old:

    Max HR = 185 bpm
    % method:  Zone 2 = 111–130 bpm

Same person with a 60 bpm resting HR (Karvonen):

    HRR = 185 − 60 = 125
    Zone 2 = (0.6 × 125 + 60) to (0.7 × 125 + 60) = 135–148 bpm

## Why train in Zone 2?

- Burns fat as the main fuel.
- Builds mitochondria and capillaries — your endurance base.
- Allows high weekly volume without frying you.

If you can't hold a conversation at Zone 2 pace, ease off — it's supposed to feel easy.
