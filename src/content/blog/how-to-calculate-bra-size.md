A correct bra size needs just **two tape measurements** and simple math. Here's the method most fitters use.

## Step 1 — Measure

- **Underbust:** snug around the ribcage, right under the bust.
- **Bust:** around the fullest part of the bust.

Measure in inches (or centimetres and divide by 2.54).

## Step 2 — Find the band

**Modern method:** round the underbust to the nearest **even** number.

    Underbust 31" → band 32
    Underbust 30" → band 30

**Traditional (+4) method:** add 4 (or 5 if odd) to the underbust:

    Underbust 31" → 31 + 5 = 36

## Step 3 — Find the cup

Subtract the band from the bust and map to a letter:

| Difference | Cup |
| --- | --- |
| 1" | A |
| 2" | B |
| 3" | C |
| 4" | D |
| 5" | DD |
| 6" | DDD / F |

## Worked example

Underbust **31"**, bust **36"**, modern method:

    Band = 32
    Difference = 36 − 32 = 4" → D cup
    Size = 32D

## Sister sizes

Sister sizes share the same cup volume: **32D = 34C = 30DD**. If a bra is snug in the band but loose in the cup, move to the next sister size. Fit in the store always beats the math — use a calculated size as the starting point, not the verdict.
