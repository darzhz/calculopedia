Your **Life Path number** is the headline number of Western numerology — the one drawn from your full **date of birth** that summarises your life's themes. Here's how to work it out.

## The formula

1. Reduce your **day** to a single digit.
2. Reduce your **month** to a single digit.
3. Reduce your **year** to a single digit.
4. Add the three, and reduce again to a single digit — unless the sum is a **master number**.

## Worked example: 15 June 1995

    Day:   1 + 5 = 6
    Month: 6
    Year:  1 + 9 + 9 + 5 = 24 → 2 + 4 = 6
    Total: 6 + 6 + 6 = 18 → 1 + 8 = 9

Life Path = **9**.

## Master numbers 11, 22 and 33

When the total is **11, 22 or 33**, numerologists don't reduce it — these are **master numbers** carrying heightened potential:

- **11** — the intuitive idealist
- **22** — the master builder
- **33** — the master teacher

For example, **18 September 1993**:

    Day:   1 + 8 = 9
    Month: 9
    Year:  1 + 9 + 9 + 3 = 22 → 2 + 2 = 4
    Total: 9 + 9 + 4 = 22 → kept as a master number

Life Path = **22**.

## A note on accuracy

There are two common methods: reducing each component first (shown here) or reducing only the total. They usually agree, but can differ on master numbers. This site uses the component method, which preserves 11, 22 and 33.
