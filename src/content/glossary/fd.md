A **Fixed Deposit (FD)** is one of the safest savings instruments in India. You deposit a lump sum for a fixed tenure (from a few months to several years) at an agreed interest rate, and the bank returns your principal plus interest at maturity. Interest is typically compounded **quarterly**.

Key points:

- Returns are **guaranteed** and fixed at the time of deposit.
- Senior citizens usually get 0.5% higher rates.
- Interest is **taxable** per your income-tax slab; banks deduct TDS above certain limits.
- A 5-year **tax-saver FD** qualifies for a Section 80C deduction.
