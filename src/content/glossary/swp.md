---
term: SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan)
description: A facility that lets you withdraw a fixed amount from a mutual fund investment at regular intervals for a regular income.
category: Finance
---

A **Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP)** lets you withdraw a fixed amount from your mutual fund investment at regular intervals — commonly monthly. It's a popular way for retirees to draw a "salary" from a lump-sum corpus while the remaining money keeps earning.

How long your money lasts depends on the corpus, the withdrawal amount and the returns:

- If your monthly withdrawal is **less** than your monthly earnings, the corpus can last indefinitely.
- If it's **more**, the corpus shrinks and eventually runs out.

Because SWPs usually run on market-linked funds, returns are not guaranteed. See how long your corpus lasts with the [SWP Calculator](/finance/swp-calculator/).
