term: CTC (Cost to Company)

**CTC (Cost to Company)** is the total your employer spends on you each year. It includes your basic salary, allowances, employer PF contribution, gratuity accrual, insurance and other benefits — not just what lands in your bank.

The journey from CTC to in-hand salary:

1. Subtract **employer PF** (12% of basic, up to the ₹15,000/month wage cap) and **gratuity accrual** to get gross salary.
2. Subtract **employee PF**, **professional tax** (~₹200/month) and **income tax** from gross.
3. Divide by 12 for the monthly in-hand figure.
