term: GST (Goods and Services Tax)

**GST** (Goods and Services Tax) is India's single indirect tax on the supply of goods and services, which replaced a tangle of earlier taxes (VAT, service tax, excise, octroi) in July 2017. It is charged at four main rates depending on the item: **5%, 12%, 18% and 28%**, plus 0% and 3% for a few special goods.

To add GST to a price: `GST = amount × rate / 100`. To extract GST from an inclusive price: `base = amount / (1 + rate / 100)`. Use the [GST Calculator](/finance/gst-calculator/) for both.
