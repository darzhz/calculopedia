---
term: BMR and TDEE
description: BMR is the calories you burn at complete rest; TDEE adds your activity level to give the calories needed to maintain your weight.
category: Health
---

**BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate)** is the energy your body uses at complete rest — breathing, heartbeat, cell repair. **TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure)** is your BMR multiplied by an activity factor (1.2 sedentary to 1.9 extra active), representing your real daily calorie burn.

To lose weight, eat roughly 500 calories below your TDEE; to gain, eat 500 above. These are estimates — real needs vary. The most accurate common BMR formula is **Mifflin-St Jeor**:

    Men: 10 × weight(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) − 5 × age + 5
    Women: 10 × weight(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) − 5 × age − 161
