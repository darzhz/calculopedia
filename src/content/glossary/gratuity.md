**Gratuity** is a lump-sum payment your employer gives you when you leave a job after **at least 5 years of continuous service**. It is governed by the **Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972**.

For employees covered by the Act, the calculation is:

    Gratuity = Last drawn (basic + DA) × completed years × 15 / 26

Gratuity received by non-government employees covered by the Act is tax-exempt up to the lowest of: the actual amount, ₹20 lakh, or the amount computed by the 15/26 formula. Estimate yours with the [Gratuity Calculator](/finance/gratuity-calculator/).
