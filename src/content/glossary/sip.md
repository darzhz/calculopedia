A **Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)** is a method of investing a fixed amount into a mutual fund at regular intervals — usually every month. Instead of investing a lump sum, you buy units of the fund on a schedule.

Because you buy at whatever the fund's NAV is each month, you automatically buy fewer units when prices are high and more when they are low. This is called **rupee-cost averaging**, and it smooths out the impact of market volatility over time.

SIPs are popular for long-term wealth building in India. Their returns depend on market performance and are **not guaranteed**. The future value of monthly investments can be estimated with the [SIP Calculator](/finance/sip-calculator/).
